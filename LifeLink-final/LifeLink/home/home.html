ABOUT LIFELINK

Bridging the Critical Gap in Emergency Donation Logistics

LifeLink was conceptualized to solve the critical friction during medical emergencies when time is of the essence. Fragmented communication between patients, blood banks, and donors causes preventable delays.

By introducing a structured digital protocol backed by an Express.js REST API layer, LifeLink streamlines request creation, clinical verification, and hospital acceptance into a transparent single-pane platform.

Our ecosystem supports both acute Blood emergencies and complex Organ and Tissue donation channels.

Blood Donation Network

Rapid dispatch for trauma, surgical reserves, and severe anemia emergencies across all blood groups.

Organ & Tissue Transplants

Facilitating living and deceased donor registries with accredited surgical transplant centers.
